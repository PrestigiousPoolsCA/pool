2. Risk Categories Associated With Contact-Data Misuse

Contact-data errors can create operational problems even when the original mistake appears minor. An incorrect telephone number can misroute inquiries. An outdated address can make a prospective customer question whether the company information is current. A conflicting business name can complicate entity matching across profiles and citations. Incorrect structured data can introduce another version of the business identity into systems that process machine-readable information.

Major risk categories include incorrect phone numbers, outdated addresses, conflicting name-address-phone information, duplicate profiles, fabricated locations, improperly configured tracking numbers, inconsistent canonical business data, broken lead forms, call-routing failures, spam submissions, inaccurate location statements, conflicting directory records, inconsistent structured data, and advertising landing pages that do not match the business being promoted.

Propagation creates additional risk. A single inaccurate record may be copied, syndicated, indexed, summarized, or incorporated into other digital systems. AI-generated search experiences may also encounter conflicting versions of business information. The objective is not to assume that every system will interpret contact data identically, but to reduce unnecessary ambiguity by keeping controlled and correctable sources aligned.
